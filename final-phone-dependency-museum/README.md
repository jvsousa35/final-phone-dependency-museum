## Museum Topic

My museum topic is **phone dependency**, presented through a digital exhibit called **The Museum of Digital Attention** with the subtitle **The Attention Trap**.

The site explores how smartphones changed from useful communication tools into systems that shape attention, habits, emotions, routines, sleep, focus, and social behavior.

The goal is not to argue that phones are evil. The goal is to help visitors understand how phone dependency forms and how attention can be protected.

---

## Main Story / Guided Experience

The museum guides visitors through five main exhibits:

1. **From Tool to Companion**  
   Explains how phones became useful, trusted, and always present.

2. **The Attention Economy**  
   Explains notifications, feeds, infinite scroll, and how apps compete for time.

3. **The Habit Loop**  
   Explains how checking becomes automatic through cues, rewards, and repetition.

4. **The Human Cost**  
   Explains how phone dependency can affect sleep, focus, school/work, relationships, and mental load.

5. **The Attention Reset**  
   Gives visitors a reflection-based ending where they make one small commitment to protect their attention.

The site is designed as a guided museum route, not as random pages.

## Design Style

The design style is **Modern Museum / Editorial**.

This style shaped the site through:

- dark museum-inspired backgrounds
- large editorial typography
- exhibit labels and captions
- quiet spacing
- structured cards
- visual artifacts
- clean navigation
- a serious but reflective tone

The goal was to make the website feel like a digital exhibit experience rather than a normal report or blog.

---

## Brand Archetype

The brand archetype is **The Sage**.

The Sage archetype fits this topic because the museum is meant to teach, explain, and help visitors understand their own behavior. The tone is reflective, serious, and educational, but not overly dramatic.

---

## Cialdini Principle

The Cialdini principle used is **Commitment and Consistency**.

The site uses small reflection prompts throughout the museum. Visitors are asked to notice their habits before being asked to make one small attention reset commitment on the final page.

This helps the visitor move from passive reading to personal reflection.

---

## AI Orchestration Process

AI was used intentionally as part of the workflow. It was not used as one giant prompt.

I used AI to help:

- break down the assignment requirements
- choose and refine the museum topic
- plan the guided route
- organize the exhibit structure
- research phone dependency and attention
- create page concepts
- write and revise exhibit text
- design the visual direction
- generate HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and README content
- improve the site so it felt more like a final-level museum experience

The final decisions, topic direction, tone, and structure were directed by me. AI helped produce and organize the work, but I reviewed and shaped the final result.
